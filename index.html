03Projects

Things I'm building because I want to understand the bottom of the stack, starting with Plasma, the quantum language, and the operating system that ships it.

Plasma

A high-level quantum computing language and deterministic statevector simulator, designed for planckOS. Line-based assembly-like syntax, reproducible output per seed, and backends from a reference interpreter to Clifford stabilizer, MPS and a PTX-only CUDA path.

  • Rust
  • Quantum
  • Simulation
  • Deterministic
2026

planckOS

A Rust-based quantum simulation OS being built entirely from scratch: custom MBR and loader, 64-bit kernel, FAT32 driver, SMP and a REPL shell, with Plasma shipped on every release.

  • Rust
  • Assembly
  • Kernel
  • FAT32
  • SMP
2026

NumCore

Bare-metal scientific calculator firmware for the ARM Cortex-M3 in Rust #![no_std], with no OS, no allocator and no standard library, plus a deterministic Q31.32 fixed-point math engine.

  • Rust
  • no_std
  • Cortex-M3
  • Fixed-Point
2025

Tungsten

A from-scratch mathematical platform for symbolic algebra, exact calculus and N-dimensional graphing, written in zero-dependency Rust. A FOSS alternative to Mathematica, WIP.

  • Rust
  • Mathematics
  • Symbolic Algebra
  • WIP
2026
